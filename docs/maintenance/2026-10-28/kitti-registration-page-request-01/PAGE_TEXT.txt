Registration
Here you can: (1) Register for Downloading KITTI, and (2) Apply for Submitting to KITTI. The second step is optional and is typically conducated at a later point in time. The second step must only be undertaken if a paper is submitted to a conference where the experimental results are ready and the conference has been determined. Applications for submission will be reviewed manually which may take up to one week.
