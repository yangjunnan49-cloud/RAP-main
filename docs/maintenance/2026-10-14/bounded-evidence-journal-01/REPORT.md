All15 predeclared tiny-byte controls pass independent byte-level decoding and complete fixture directory reconciliation. This is a new artifact-only prototype, not a whole-dossier guarantee or old archive retry.

No refunds after partial failure. Both per-channel and global budgets must pass. Rejected logs are not silently truncated or called complete: the journal fails and later body writes are refused. Reserved footer space does not guarantee successful I/O, crash durability or power-loss atomicity; a missing/bad footer remains unsealed.
