# Camera admission decision

Decision now: CONDITIONAL_SOURCE_AND_OBSERVATION_GATE, numerical image/model work NOT_READY. This matrix makes the next strategy reviewable without inventing an executable experiment before its inputs exist. It supplements, rather than changes, the six closed scopes.

| Required question | Established now | Still required before the dependent claim |
|---|---|---|
| Are all requested images available? |273 camera0 anchors identified across all200 pairs; known local paths absent and owner has no other path|Legitimate complete packet and exact member/source identities; no favorable subset|
| Is projection tied to actual acquisition? |Provider contract and two local calibration identities; intended timestamp/raw mapping|Actual dimensions, image/calibration/timing association and realized extraction record; provider prose alone is insufficient|
| Is there useful image evidence? |Only a supplied-reference forward-depth ceiling|Actual image support, static correspondences and geometric information; positive depth is not a match|
| Can the estimator supply the pose quantity being evaluated? |No visual estimator has been selected or run|Declare transform direction, scale source, observable degrees of freedom, degeneracy/no-evidence handling and reference-free inference inputs before selecting an error metric|
| Does RAP add value beyond ordinary geometry? |No camera comparison or increment exists|Common-input ordinary visual geometry and existing LiDAR controls, plus a separately declared RAP initializer; measure the estimator, not a surrogate similarity score|
| Is camera/RAP coordinate coupling exact? |The point-direction-dependent correction is not a global SE(3) map|Retained original point identities and explicit observation/geometry roles; any initializer approximation evaluated directly, without an invented fixed conjugation|
| Can absence of support reject a pose? |No:08 no-support includes7/17 correct accepted and2/10 wrong accepted|Explicit no-evidence fallback; no hard veto inferred from these post-hoc counts|
| Is a new physical/generalization result admitted? |No; F2 scene/cohort and references are exposed|Separately authenticated independent acquisition/cohort and a prospective evaluation design; newly viewed camera pixels do not make the already exposed cohort independent|
| Is an online/system gain admitted? |No; no new system run or timing exists|A separately bounded downstream test with accuracy protection, coverage, fallback, actual latency and attribution; an offline pair result alone cannot supply it|

Do not choose thresholds from the current support counts, replace missing images with synthetic appearance, or train a gate to reproduce historical correctness labels. Any later experiment must first state a result that could stop the branch. If actual observations cannot support a scale/pose-aware ordinary estimator, the next decision is to stop or redefine the task explicitly, not silently lower its claim or rescue it with reference information. No package, model, source access or numerical payload is authorized by this decision document alone.
