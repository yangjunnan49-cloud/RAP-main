# Primary-source literature matrix

Access date: 2026-10-12, Asia/Shanghai. This is a targeted broad review of the current RAP-SLAM bottlenecks, not an exhaustive novelty search. Paper mechanisms, author documentation and local applicability are separated. No headline score below is treated as a directly comparable RAP-SLAM result.

The review covers six tracks: system constraints, measured geometry, generative registration, lightweight learning, differentiable numerical methods, and independent observations. Full relevant sections were inspected for the central papers; peripheral works were screened through their primary abstracts or author repositories. Installation and inference were not performed.

| ID | Primary work / source | Relevant mechanism | Consequence for this project |
|---|---|---|---|
| P01 | [RAP, v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2512.01850v2) | Joint registered-point generation, rigidity forcing and rigid pose recovery | Multi-view generation and use as a downstream optimizer initializer already belong to RAP. Paper and local 12-layer checkpoint conventions differ; membership is unresolved. |
| P02 | [Rectified Point Flow](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.05282), [author code](https://github.com/GradientSpaces/Rectified-Point-Flow) | Conditional point velocity fields for registration and assembly | Training code provides a reference for flow objectives, but does not make large-scene retraining cheap or prove local adaptation works. |
| P03 | [KISS-SLAM](https://arxiv.org/html/2503.12660v1), [author repository](https://github.com/PRBonn/kiss-slam) | Local-map loops, geometric validation and fine-grained pose graph correction | Appropriate full-system reference. Scan-level correction and submap loops are established engineering, not new RAP contributions. Pin the paper tag rather than moving main for future comparisons. |
| P04 | [Information-aware odometry and retroactive loops, 2026](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.13516v1) | Samples a rematched ICP cost landscape; geometry-dependent graph weighting; hierarchical and retroactive loops | Especially close prior art. Generic information weighting or recovering missed loops is insufficient novelty. Curvature remains an approximation, not an automatically calibrated covariance. |
| P05 | [GLIM](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.10344), [LiDAR-only documentation](https://koide3.github.io/glim/quickstart.html) | Direct multi-scan matching cost factors and global submap optimization | Official LiDAR-only mode exists. Earlier blanket IMU exclusion is too broad. Published inertial results still cannot be imported into a LiDAR-only comparison. |
| P06 | [BALM2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2209.08854), [author code](https://github.com/hku-mars/BALM) | Point clusters, analytic second-order derivatives and elimination of geometric nuisance parameters | Explains efficient BA construction and close prior art to our shared-plane helpers. Does not reopen the stopped MF0 objective. |
| P07 | [MAD-BA](https://arxiv.org/html/2501.03972v2), [author code](https://github.com/rvp-group/mad-ba) | Joint surfel/pose optimization with a sensing uncertainty model | A stronger observation model than equal-mass shared planes. Its reference/association caveats matter; a lower residual still cannot validate physical truth in our cases. |
| P08 | [MAD-ICP](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.05828), [author code](https://github.com/rvp-group/mad-icp) | Structural matching and informed local-map management | Useful control for local odometry. Wholesale substitution might improve engineering but would not isolate RAP value. |
| P09 | [GenZ-ICP](https://arxiv.org/html/2411.06766v1), [author code](https://github.com/cocel-postech/genz-icp) | Adaptive combination of point-to-plane and point-to-point objectives | Strong degeneracy control, not evidence of guaranteed KITTI improvement. Its reported KITTI comparison places KISS-ICP ahead; source also describes dataset tuning. |
| P10 | [X-ICP](https://arxiv.org/abs/2211.16335) | Directional localizability detection and constrained updates | An observable-direction gate has substantial prior art. Degeneracy and repetitive-scene ambiguity are distinct failure modes. |
| P11 | [MapClosures author repository](https://github.com/PRBonn/MapClosures) | Density-map place recognition and geometric loop proposals | A suitable independent proposal control for a new submap experiment, rather than a new method by itself. Existing LC0 single-keyframe placement stop stays intact. |
| P12 | [VGGT-SLAM 2.0](https://arxiv.org/html/2601.19887v1), [author code](https://github.com/MIT-SPARK/VGGT-SLAM) | Graph design respecting model ambiguity and attention-based loop verification | The transferable lesson is to design around the model's actual ambiguity. Copying SL(4) into metric LiDAR RAP would add unsupported degrees of freedom. Attention verification is already prior art. |
| P13 | [MMD-Reg, ICML 2026](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v306/crane26a.html), [full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.27818v1), [author code](https://github.com/csiro-robotics/mmd-reg) | Smooth distribution matching with random features, LM and implicit derivatives | New useful solver candidate. Low-overlap neural tests use learned overlap masks; plain MMD is not a demonstrated cure for our low-overlap or alias cases. |
| P14 | [BUFFER-X extended paper, 2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.02759), [author code](https://github.com/MIT-SPARK/BUFFER-X) | Geometric scale selection, multiscale descriptors and hierarchical matching | A distinct strong descriptor/registration control. Automatic scales and multiscale matching are established; our fixed P0/FPFH negatives do not evaluate this whole method. |
| P15 | [GeoTransformer](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2022/html/Qin_Geometric_Transformer_for_Fast_and_Robust_Point_Cloud_Registration_CVPR_2022_paper.html) | Rigid-invariant geometric context for superpoint matching | Already tested locally on online candidates without material system gain. Replacing the matcher alone has low demonstrated value under that fixed interface. |
| P16 | [Deep Global Registration](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content_CVPR_2020/html/Choy_Deep_Global_Registration_CVPR_2020_paper.html) | Learned correspondence confidence, differentiable rigid solve and SE(3) refinement | Close prior art against a generic learned confidence plus pose refinement claim. |
| P17 | [REGTR](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2022/html/Yew_REGTR_End-to-End_Point_Cloud_Correspondences_With_Transformers_CVPR_2022_paper.html) | Transformer prediction of overlap and corresponding positions | Learned correspondences from transformer tokens are established, and RLP2 already gives a local negative result for one frozen-token construction. |
| P18 | [RPM-Net](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content_CVPR_2020/html/Yew_RPM-Net_Robust_Point_Matching_Using_Learned_Features_CVPR_2020_paper.html) | Hybrid features, soft assignment and learned annealing | Soft matching and learned solver schedules are not new. They also depend on the learning/data regime. |
| P19 | [PointDSC](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2021/html/Bai_PointDSC_Robust_Point_Cloud_Registration_Using_Deep_Spatial_Consistency_CVPR_2021_paper.html) | Learned spatial consistency and spectral correspondence filtering | A spatial compatibility selector alone has mature prior art and cannot create information absent from proposed matches. |
| P20 | [Point-TTA](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/ICCV2023/html/Hatem_Point-TTA_Test-Time_Adaptation_for_Point_Cloud_Registration_Using_Multitask_Meta-Auxiliary_ICCV_2023_paper.html) | Meta-trained auxiliary tasks supporting test-time adaptation | Ordinary TTA is not novel; its meta-training preparation differs from simply fitting our frozen model at test time. |
| P21 | [DiffusionReg author implementation](https://github.com/Jiang-HB/DiffusionReg) | SE(3) diffusion and pose refinement for object registration | Lie-group generative refinement is established. Object-scale results do not imply outdoor loop or trajectory benefits. |
| P22 | [MinBackProp](https://arxiv.org/html/2404.17993v1) | Implicit differentiation of minimal solver conditions | A numerical alternative to generic solver autograd, requiring identifiability and independent derivatives. Does not diagnose the NOC4 mismatch. |
| P23 | [Deep Declarative Networks](https://arxiv.org/abs/1909.04866) | Implicitly defined optimization layers | Optimizer-in-the-network is prior art. Regularity and converged solution assumptions need checking. |
| P24 | [Continuous rotation representations](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content_CVPR_2019/html/Zhou_On_the_Continuity_of_Rotation_Representations_in_Neural_Networks_CVPR_2019_paper.html) | Continuous 5D/6D rotation representations | Useful for global rotation regression. A bounded local Lie residual has a different role; representation changes alone are not innovation. |
| P25 | [MapMOS author code](https://github.com/PRBonn/MapMOS) | Moving-object segmentation and volumetric static/dynamic beliefs | Independent learned motion evidence is distinct from C5's geometric persistence mask, but adds a model/dependency burden and is not novel simply as preprocessing. |
| P26 | [ICP covariance analysis](https://arxiv.org/abs/1410.7632) | Explains why rematching matters for covariance estimates | Do not call an inverse point-to-point Hessian calibrated uncertainty. |
| P27 | [GTSAM adjoints and covariances](https://gtsam.org/2021/02/23/uncertainties-part3.html) | Frame-consistent covariance transport and composition | Aggregation needs its perturbation convention and correlations. Scan-count weighting does not supply missing uncertainty. |

## Implementation and metric sources

| ID | Primary source | Relevant constraint |
|---|---|---|
| T01 | [small_gicp](https://github.com/koide3/small_gicp) | CPU fine registration with Python bindings; exposes pose, inliers and final Hessian. The package itself excludes GPU implementations. |
| T02 | [gtsam_points](https://github.com/koide3/gtsam_points) | Direct ICP/GICP/VGICP and BA factors. Current dependencies/version choices are not a drop-in guarantee for our installed GTSAM4.2/Blackwell environment. |
| T03 | [PyTorch2.7 SDPA](https://docs.pytorch.org/docs/2.7/generated/torch.nn.functional.scaled_dot_product_attention.html) | Kernel selection and arithmetic can affect numerical behavior; diagnostics must record backend and layout rather than assume a cause. |
| T04 | [PyTorch2.7 SVD](https://docs.pytorch.org/docs/2.7/generated/torch.linalg.svd.html) | Spectral gaps affect generic U/V gradients. NOC4 already checks selected spectral validity; this warning alone does not establish its failure cause. |
| T05 | [PyTorch2.7 reproducibility](https://docs.pytorch.org/docs/2.7/notes/randomness.html) | Seed control alone does not guarantee identical results across every platform, release or operation. |
| T06 | [Official KITTI odometry evaluation](https://www.cvlibs.net/datasets/kitti/eval_odometry.php) | Segment relative drift and global aligned ATE should be reported separately; none of the pairwise success thresholds substitutes for system evaluation. |

## Reading and claim limits

No third-party paper notes, blogs, generated summaries, citation counts or incomparable leaderboard scores are used to justify the ranking. Search results from such pages were discarded. The GenZ v2 HTML endpoint was unavailable; the accessible v1 paper and current author repository were used and are explicitly versioned here. Dynamic repository pages were inspected as current documentation; future implementation must pin actual source and weights.

Novelty judgments are targeted comparisons against these close mechanisms. They are not proof that no other paper implements a proposed component. A later selected design needs a narrow final novelty search after its equations and ablations are fixed.
