# Partial-overlap measurement contract v1: diagnostic specification

Status: design and counterexample validation complete; **no deployable acceptance rule**. Research remains unresolved; manuscript remains paused. This contract records what evidence would be needed and which existing quantities cannot supply it.

## Four distinct obligations

| Obligation | Required content | Current state |
|---|---|---|
| Coordinate and source identity | Exact scan IDs/point bytes, units, transform direction, calibration, acquisition and pose times, preprocessing and pose-reference provenance | Point-byte/input direction arithmetic checked; physical timing/calibration/reference precision unverified |
| Fit to a partial support | Candidate-independent putative matches, explicit residual model, unassigned points/outliers retained, source/target identities and support extent | PCS1 0.6m point-to-point residuals reproducible on fixed hypotheses; no claim these are genuine associations |
| Conditional local constraint | Stated perturbation convention, metric scale, null directions and spatial support under fixed associations | MCV1 conditional normal matrices measured and audited; no calibrated noise model/covariance |
| Global place and association identity | A defined test against physically plausible coherent wrong associations, including repeated structures and wrong transforms on true loops | No verified mechanism yet; local fit/rank alone fail the synthetic and real sufficiency checks |

Every candidate currently carries `physical_identity=UNVERIFIED` and `acceptance=UNDETERMINED`. A consumer must not map either to acceptance. `NO_EVIDENCE` means a measurement is unavailable; it is not a negative correctness label. Fit/rank results are diagnostics and do not establish physical reference truth.

## Measurement definitions

For putative pairs `(x_k,y_k)`, apply the stored `T_i_to_j` exactly: `q_k=R*x_k+t`; residual `||q_k-y_k||`. Inlier means residual strictly below 0.6m, inherited from PCS1 rather than optimized here. Keep rejected pair rows and total match count. A partial-support residual does not score the entire unmatched scene as erroneous. Conversely, discarding unmatched structure can hide a wrong repeated motif; it cannot be used as evidence of identity.

Center retained q at c and preserve radius `r=sqrt(mean(||q-c||²))` in meters. Normalize `v=(q-c)/r` for nonzero radius and form `J=[I,-skew(v)]`, `H=mean(JᵀJ)`. H describes conditional linearized point-to-point constraints for a translation at the centroid and an angular perturbation scaled by r. It has no noise scale, no validated independent-observation model and no statistical coverage interpretation. It must not be inverted and passed to PGO as a certified information matrix.

The centered exact-arithmetic identity is `H=diag(I, tr(S)*I-S)` with `S=mean(v*vᵀ)`, and `tr(S)=1` when r>0. The rotational block has eigenvalues `1-s_i`. Noncollinear point-to-point supports give full rank, including a plane; a line leaves one rotation unconstrained and coincident points leave all three. Repeating an identical noncollinear motif or coherently shifting all false associations preserves these ranks. This algebra explains why full rank need not improve PCS1's count-only diagnostic. The real study verifies this finite conclusion without claiming that every possible spectral statistic is useless.

For point-to-plane residuals, the Jacobian is instead `[nᵀ,(v cross n)ᵀ]`; one plane leaves three directions unobserved. The residual model must be named before discussing degeneracy. Normal estimation, normal uncertainty, robust-weight derivatives and covariance are outside MCV1.

## Requirements for a future acceptance mechanism

Freeze a mechanism and its physically meaningful error budget before confirmation. It must state how independent place/association evidence is obtained, what happens to partial/unassigned support, what rival transforms are tested, and why absent rivals in a finite bank do not certify uniqueness. Reject-all remains a baseline. Ordinary and RAP candidates receive the same evidence and access to raw inputs.

Preserve primary R<=5deg/t<=1m and secondary2m development labels. Report incorrect transforms on true loops separately from nonloops. Predeclare correct-accept gain, new wrong accepts, lost correct accepts and downstream trajectory effects. Existing development IDs and scan-connected components cannot be renamed unseen tests. Physical session/route separation and confirmation sample sufficiency are required for generalization claims.

Before real confirmation, preserve counterexamples for coherent false correspondence, repeated motifs, line/near-line support, point-to-point versus point-to-plane plane support, low overlap and wrong transform on a true loop. A successful robust optimization certificate, when available, certifies the stated objective under its assumptions; physical association identity needs its own justification. [TEASER](https://arxiv.org/abs/2001.07715) explicitly formulates robust registration using a truncated least squares objective and certifies optimization. The identity distinction here is our inference from that objective and our counterexamples, not a claim that its authors certify physical loop identity.

Current same-cloud, same-match splits cannot resolve an exactly coherent alias by themselves. They are stability controls when used, and need a stated independent source if promoted to evidence about place identity. This does not establish that new sensors are universally necessary.
